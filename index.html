+ THE ANSWERS

Asked every week.

What does a commercial roof cost in Tampa?

Re-roofs run $5.50–$9.00 per square foot depending on tear-off, insulation, and how many curbs and penetrations the roof carries. Restorations run $2.90–$4.50. Square footage, access, and deck condition move the number — which is why every quote starts with a walk-through, not a ballpark.

How long will our building be a construction site?

Most 10,000–20,000 sq ft re-roofs run 5–8 working days. We stage around your hours — materials crane-lifted before opening, hot work during low-traffic hours. Your doors stay open.

Can you tell if we need a re-roof or just a restoration?

Yes — that's the core sample and infrared scan. Dry insulation and an attached membrane: restoration buys 10–15 years for roughly a third of a re-roof. Saturated insulation: coating over it is throwing money at a sponge. We show you the cores, then quote.

Do you work around our business hours?

Yes. Most of our work is staged early, late, or weekends — warehouses keep shipping, offices keep working. The walk-through itself takes 45 minutes and we never need your staff.

What's a maintenance program, and is it worth it?

Two documented inspections a year, drains cleared, seams probed, repairs priced before they're leaks. It's how a roof hits 25 years instead of 14. Members get priority scheduling after storms.
